# Questions ledger

Questions are more valuable than a long list of future implementations. Give each question a scope and a status.

| Question | Status | What would count as progress? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What physical processes determine a resting membrane potential? | Milestone 1 | Explain ions, permeability, and why LIF abstracts them away. |
| What does the LIF leak term mean mathematically and physically? | Milestone 1 | Derive the sign of the restoring term and predict its equilibrium. |
| What makes a spike train an event sequence rather than a continuous activation? | Milestone 1 | Explain threshold, reset, refractory time, and lost subthreshold detail. |
| What is a neural code? | Planned | State a stimulus, response variable, noise model, and a decoding/encoding question. |
| Can firing rate and spike timing carry different information? | Planned | Design a simulated comparison before measuring it. |
| When does a biological observation improve an artificial system? | Long-term | Specify a transferable abstraction and an ordinary AI baseline. |
