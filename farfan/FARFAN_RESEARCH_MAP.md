## Historical and current work must remain separate

### Historical tactile-coding lineage

The 2006–2013 vibrissal work concerns rat tactile sensing and afferent/multi-unit recordings. It is the closest direct local bridge to the planned “stimulus to spike” and later neural-coding modules. It should not be described as evidence about human visual prostheses.

### Current listed neuroprosthesis work

The official CONICET topic concerns electrophysiological validation of an intracortical visual neuroprosthesis in blind participants. A 2022 *Journal of Neural Engineering* paper examined six-month signal stability/connectivity from a 96-channel visual-cortex array; a 2025 *Cerebral Cortex* paper studied changes in resting-state connectivity in blind neuroprosthesis users. These are much closer to human chronic implants than to scalp EEG, but are not equivalent to single-neuron coding experiments and do not establish a general learning mechanism.

## The intellectual path this supports

```text
physical texture / stimulus
  → receptor and peripheral afferent activity
  → event/spike-derived representation
  → information measure and decoder
  → careful biomimetic abstraction
  → technological test against an ordinary engineering baseline
```

The first milestone touches only the leftmost conceptual preparation: membrane dynamics and spikes. Do not claim continuity from a LIF notebook to the group’s empirical work until you can state the intervening measurement, coding, and inference assumptions.

## Appropriate first conversation

Ask, rather than assume: whether attending/cursar the course as an `alumno vocacional` is possible; what current computational or data-analysis tasks would be useful under supervision; and which one of the papers below would be an appropriate first reading. Bring a short description of the completed EEG project and ask for methodological criticism.
